Front + Rear Parking Cameras

2013 Nissan 370Z NISMO · Kenwood DMX7706S · Planning / not purchased · Updated October 11, 2026

Shopping shortlist

  1. Front curb camera: DS18 AHDRC — approx. $30. Adjustable compact front/rear camera kit; mount discreetly in front grille aimed at curb. Manufacturer / kit details. Confirm CVBS mode and included cable before checkout.
  2. Rear camera: aSATAH Nissan 370Z plate-light replacement, 8 LED version — approx. $20–30. Find on Amazon. Check 2013 370Z lamp housing fit, plate illumination, and NTSC/CVBS RCA output before ordering.
  3. Installation consumables — approx. $10–15: appropriately sized add-a-fuse, inline fuse as needed, waterproof heat-shrink splices, wire, loom, zip ties and mounting fasteners. Match fuse tap to actual fuse-box slot.
  4. RCA extensions — only if kit-supplied video cables are too short; route through existing grommets and away from heat/moving parts.

Estimated parts budget: $60–75 if both kits include adequate cables; excludes shipping, tax and any extras.

Installation plan

  • Front: recessed in lower grille, with clear view of pavement immediately ahead of NISMO lip; ignition-switched fused power and ground.
  • Rear: plate-light area; preserve functional license plate lighting; rear video triggered by reverse.
  • Use Kenwood's front and rear camera video inputs. Existing radio harness was connected during stereo installation; no new radio harness or separate monitor planned.
  • Test camera picture, image orientation, switching, and parking guidelines before final trim reassembly. Do not use Maestro reverse trigger as camera power.

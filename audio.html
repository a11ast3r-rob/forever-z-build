SYSTEM MAP

Signal and power layout

1 · KenwoodSource, EQ, preamp outputs and remote turn-on from the dash.
2 · RCA / remoteFront, rear and sub signal runs from the dash to the amp location behind the passenger seat.
3 · NVX 5-channelChannels 1–4 handle cabin speakers; channel 5 is the dedicated bass channel.
4 · Front stageKICKER component woofers + tweeters, installed with verified polarity.
5 · L7T 10Loaded 2-ohm hatch enclosure becomes the real low-bass stage; the Bose sub is no longer the long-term plan.
Amp location is now locked: behind the passenger seat on the acrylic mounting panel. Main 4-ga power and ground terminate there; RCA and remote runs come from the Kenwood side of the car. Keep the main fuse near the battery, not buried deep in the cabin.

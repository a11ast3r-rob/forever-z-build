THE ACTUAL BUILD

More than a head unit

Factory Bose removed

Factory amp and speakers came out. We routed aftermarket RCA and remote signals while adapting suitable OEM speaker wires and confirming polarity.

Hidden hardware

NVX mounted behind the passenger seat on an acrylic panel, with 4-gauge fused power and ground and signal cables routed through the console.

Subwoofer redemption

The used KICKER L7T failed testing even though channel 5 worked. After its return, we bought the L7S loaded 10 locally. It works — and it hits.

Clean beats loud

Front imaging and controlled bass matter more than maximum volume. More tuning, hum tracing and a possible custom top-firing box are still ahead.

# Build Decisions

Updated **2026-09-28**.

## Locked principles

- Reliable project-daily, not a race-car teardown.
- Clean/deep sound; no SPL chase.
- Inspect before replacing.
- Real-car evidence outranks generic Z advice.
- Keep factory NISMO parts with the car when major upgrades happen.
- Air suspension is long-term, after chassis health.
- Preserve hatch usability and service access.

## Current decisions

### Diagnostics
- Standalone handheld ANCEL-style scanner; no required phone app.
- Do not clear CEL until exact code/freeze-frame evidence is saved.
- Driver-side catalyst is **not yet declared failed**.
- If failure is confirmed after upstream causes are ruled out, Fast Intentions Resonated High Flow Cats are the leading replacement direction.

### Maintenance
- Cheap first spend: cabin/engine filters and belt by condition.
- PCV is an age/mileage inspection/renewal candidate.
- 2013 NISMO plugs: 60k/48-month Nissan interval; verify ~120k service.
- NISMO brake-fluid service follows R35 Special II factory specification.

### Audio
- DMX809S ordered.
- KICKER 51KSS6504 fronts ordered/inbound.
- NVX NDA11005 selected.
- NVX XKIT46 selected.
- Keep Bose sub temporarily.
- Phase-1 treatment: butyl + 1/8-inch CCF + roller + Tesa 51608; no MLV yet.

### Chassis
- Wheels-off health inspection before suspension purchases.
- Cheap sway hardware only if worn.
- Rear diff bushing is a high-priority inspection, not an automatic solid-bushing conversion.
- Repair confirmed wear before tires/alignment.

### Cooling
- ~220°F oil temperature is logged, not treated as emergency.
- OEM-style oil-pressure monitoring is high on longevity list.
- Thermostatic 25-row oil cooler remains later/post-baseline.

### Air
- Vehicle-specific divorced rear remains default.
- ALP4 leading management direction; optional height sensing.
- Hidden, serviceable hatch-floor install with rubber isolation and sound treatment.

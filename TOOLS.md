Rule: **buy tools that create reusable capability; borrow/rent specialized tools that may only be used once.** Safety and accurate diagnosis come before convenience.

## Tier 3 — Diagnose only if the baseline gives us a reason

Do not buy all of this preemptively.

- [ ] Cylinder leak-down tester

- [ ] Fuel-pressure test equipment if symptoms require it
- [ ] Smoke machine for intake / vacuum leak diagnosis
- [ ] Cooling-system vacuum-fill tool if we begin doing repeated coolant work

### Leak-down note
The small portable air tank may be useful for brief checks, but a leak-down test is more consistent with a regulated compressor that can maintain stable pressure. We do **not** need to buy a compressor just to perform the arrival compression test; the compression tester uses the engine's own cranking pressure.

## Don't buy yet

Wait until a real job proves the need:

- engine hoist / stand
- transmission jack
- press
- welder
- large shop compressor
- advanced scan / scope equipment
- specialty Nissan timing / engine tools

If we ever reach a job where one of those becomes repeatedly useful, then it becomes a garage-capability purchase rather than a one-job expense.

## Arrival minimum

If the car arrived tomorrow, the most important missing capability would be:

**floor jack + 4 stands + chocks + torque wrench + hand tools + OBD scanner + compression tester + battery charger + fluid-service tools + good lighting.**

That gets us through the majority of the 144k baseline without paying a shop for every inspection step.

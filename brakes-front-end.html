CHECK PAD BOX

Shims, clips and pad pins

The factory procedure calls for new shims with new pads. Confirm the chosen sets include compatible shims; buy separate correct hardware if needed. Inspect retaining pins, cross springs and clips for damage or corrosion; replace as needed. Do not omit a retaining clip.

MEASURE FIRST

Rotors and brake fluid

No rotors ordered yet. Check each rotor with a micrometer and inspect its surface. Pad-only work does not require disconnecting hydraulic lines. If hydraulic service is necessary, use Nissan's NISMO-specified R35 Special II brake fluid and the factory bleeding procedure.

Essential tools

  • Jack, rated jack stands, wheel chocks, lug socket and torque wrench.
  • Rotor micrometer; dial indicator if checking runout.
  • Pad/piston spreader suitable for multi-piston fixed calipers.
  • Small pliers, pin punch suitable for pad pins, brake-safe cleaner, clean rags and protective equipment.

Consumables

  • Correct new shims; any worn retaining clips/pins/springs.
  • Factory-approved/suitable high-temp brake hardware lubricant, only on the specified contact surfaces.
  • Do not put lubricant on pads' friction faces or rotors.
  • Optional: notebook or photos before disassembly.

Use a brake rotor micrometer at multiple points around the swept friction area, away from the unworn outer ridge. Surface smoothness by touch cannot establish safe rotor thickness, thickness variation or runout. Runout requires a properly mounted rotor and a dial indicator. Any cracked, badly heat-damaged, excessively thin or badly uneven rotor needs corrective action. For refinishing, Nissan requires adequate remaining thickness above the wear limit.

DIY WORKFLOW

Garage day — in order

Safety hold: If braking pull becomes strong or unpredictable, there's a leak, piston won't retract smoothly, severe drag, damaged retaining hardware or a soft pedal, stop and repair/seek professional help before road use. Never work under a car supported only by a floor jack. Do not blow brake dust with compressed air or press the pedal while pads are removed.
Stage, photograph and secure

Work on level ground. Chock wheels, release parking brake as needed only when the vehicle is safely secured, loosen lug nuts before lifting, and support on approved jack points/stands. Remove one wheel. Photograph the assembly and mark the old pads by corner and inner/outer position.

Before taking pads out: inspect what caused the pull

Compare left and right front rotors and old pads for unusually uneven wear. Inspect the four-piston front calipers for leaks, boot damage, rubbing or heat signs; inspect brake hoses. Spin wheels as appropriate to compare drag, safely accounting for driveline/parking-brake effects. Note any difference.

Check the brake-fluid reservoir

Inspect level and condition. When pistons are retracted the level rises; prevent overflow onto paint. Do not open hydraulic lines for a normal pad swap. If fluid needs changing, use the 2013 NISMO R35 Special II specification—not generic DOT 3 substitutions.

Remove front pads — fixed 4-piston Akebono

Remove the small retaining clips from pad pins. Hold down the cross spring, withdraw pad pins, lift out the cross spring, then remove pads and shims. Document the order and orientation. Inspect piston boots and pad wear. Nissan BR-40–41.

Measure the front rotors and check piston movement

Clean appropriately, measure rotor thickness around the swept area, inspect for cracking, hotspots, scoring and uneven lips. Use a proper piston-spreader to retract pistons slowly/evenly while watching reservoir level. If a piston binds or pad wear is abnormal, pause and diagnose the caliper/hose before installing new pads.

Install front pads and hardware

Install correct new shims on new pads, applying only specified brake lubricant where the Nissan manual calls for it. Seat pads, install upper pin, locate cross spring, install lower pin, then fully lock BOTH retaining clips. Verify clips and pins are fully engaged; Nissan warns a loose clip could let a pad or pin fall out. BR-41.

Repeat rear pads — fixed 2-piston Akebono

Rear follows the same basic pin + cross-spring method, with inner/outer shims and shim covers specific to the rear. Inspect and measure the rear rotors; retract pistons appropriately; install correct new shims/hardware and secure both clips. Follow Nissan BR-53–55. Do not disturb the separate parking brake unless inspection calls for it.

Check everything BEFORE driving

Confirm all clips/pins, nothing contacting rotors, no leaks, and wheels torqued to Nissan spec with a torque wrench. Pump the brake pedal until firm before attempting to move. Check fluid level, confirm normal pedal and brake release, then follow the pad maker's bedding instructions in a safe area. Recheck for pull or drag at low speed.

This is a garage overview, not a substitute for the manufacturer's full illustrated service procedure. For the front/rear fixed calipers use the 4-piston/2-piston sections of the 2013 BR manual, not the base-model sliding-caliper instructions.

WHEELS ALREADY OFF

Suspension & steering inspection

Suspension play checks must be done with the correct support/load condition for the joint being tested; a loose wheel isn't proof of a bad control arm. Photograph torn bushings or any measurable movement. Final torque of rubber-bushed suspension pivots is done at specified normal ride-height position, following the factory procedure.

SPEND IN THE RIGHT ORDER

What to buy AFTER inspection

NOWBrakes first

Purchase front/rear Akebono pads, any missing new shims/retaining hardware, and rotor measuring tool. Reuse rotors only if thickness and condition pass. A braking pull needs caliper/hose/suspension investigation even after pads.

IF WORNComplete front lower arms — NISMO OEM

Driver 54501-1EA3B; passenger 54500-1EA3B. Full assemblies avoid pressing bushings in the garage; inspect first. Z1 option ↗

IF WORNFront upper control arms — stock/equivalent

Quality stock-style assemblies make the most sense for cost if worn. Z1 forged adjustable Street arms are a pricier optional upgrade, not necessary for restoring present height. Verify 2013 NISMO fitment, included ball joints and mounting hardware before purchasing. Z1 adjustable option ↗

WATCHShocks, springs, and mounts

Car sits level; no visible sag reported. Original NISMO ride is naturally firm. Keep existing springs and height; replace worn shocks only after inspection. TEIN EnduraPro Plus VSK04-B1DS2 is a stock-height damping candidate, not an approved purchase yet.

LATERAlignment

After any confirmed steering/control-arm repairs, get a four-wheel alignment at the current ride height; inspect tire condition first. Do not align around loose bushings or unsafe brakes.

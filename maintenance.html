CHEAP CHASSIS ITEMS

Inspect before ordering

Sway hardware

Low-cost bushings/end links are worthwhile only when rubber, boots or play show wear.

Diff bushing

High-priority 370Z inspection for dark fluid streaking, tearing or excess movement. Common failure, not a quick job.

Brake feedback

Inspect pads, rotors/runout, caliper hardware, hoses and front control-arm bushings before buying brake parts.

HOLD

Do not parts-cannon

O2 sensors · cats · coils · MAF sensors · control arms · shocks · wheel bearings · radiator · oil cooler all wait for evidence.

Factory references

# Current Roadmap

Updated **2026-09-28**.

## Phase A — active now: diagnose + baseline

1. Buy/use standalone handheld ANCEL scanner.
2. Save CEL code(s), freeze frame and readiness before clearing.
3. Compare bank live data and rule out upstream causes before blaming the driver-side converter.
4. Complete cheap baseline maintenance: filters, belt by condition, PCV inspection and service-history proof.
5. Verify NISMO plug history; factory interval is 60k / 48 months.
6. Run chassis health day with wheels off.
7. Finish inbound radio/front-speaker work and sound treatment.

## Phase B — reliable daily

- Repair confirmed chassis/brake/steering wear.
- Reset unknown fluid clocks as appropriate.
- Continental DWS06 Plus 245/40ZR19 front / 285/35ZR19 rear.
- Four-wheel alignment after real steering/suspension repairs.
- Resolve catalyst issue only if diagnosis confirms converter or another root cause.

## Phase C — longevity

- OEM-style oil-pressure monitoring.
- Cooling-system refresh by condition.
- Thermostatic 25-row oil cooler after baseline data.
- Preserve healthy factory radiator/fans/exchanger.

## Phase D — comfort / fun

- Finish NVX amplification/wiring after confirmed purchase.
- Tune front stage.
- Keep Bose sub until it is actually the weak link.
- Front/rear cameras.
- Optional exhaust work only as supported by CEL/catalyst diagnosis.

## Phase E — air / transformation

- Healthy chassis first.
- Vehicle-specific front + divorced rear air hardware.
- ALP4 direction with optional height sensing.
- Hidden under-hatch management and sound treatment.
- Rubber-isolated compressor mounting.
- Final camber/toe correction and alignment at normal drive height.
- Later wheel/sub packaging around final air layout.

## Hold list

Do not rush:
- replacement cats,
- O2 sensors,
- random suspension arms,
- shocks without test evidence,
- wheel bearings without symptoms,
- big radiator,
- full air system,
- 18-inch wheels,
- future sub stage.

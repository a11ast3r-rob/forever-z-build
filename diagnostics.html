SCANNER

ANCEL is now part of the baseline

Confirmed tool

The standalone ANCEL handheld is in use. It has already exported P0420/P0430. Next useful captures are Mode $06 values, freeze frame, readiness, fuel trims and any available misfire data.

Clearing rule

Yes, a code can be cleared after the evidence is saved. Do not repeatedly erase the CEL just to keep the dash clean: clearing resets readiness and can hide the pattern we are trying to measure. Clear once after baseline work, then let the car prove what returns.

TEMPORARY DRIVING RULE

Can it be driven while we finish the diagnosis?

Reasonable short-term use

If the CEL is steady, the engine is smooth, power feels normal, temperatures stay normal and there is no strong sulfur smell or converter rattle, use it gently while we finish the baseline. Avoid repeated hard pulls and unnecessary high-load driving.

Stop driving / reassess

Do not keep driving through a flashing CEL, active misfire, major power loss, severe heat, glowing/rattling converter, or strong rotten-egg/sulfur smell. A restricted or overheated converter can turn a manageable repair into a larger one.

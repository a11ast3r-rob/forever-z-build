2018 front + underbody damage

CARFAX specifically reports minor front/undercarriage damage on 07/05/2018. Inspect the NISMO low aero, undertray, front supports/subframe, oil pan, steering/rack area, exhaust and old alignment/repair clues.

Brake history is old on paper

Front/rear pads were documented at 55,665 miles; brake fluid at 73,527. Hard-braking feedback therefore gets a measurement-driven brake + front-suspension inspection, not a guess.

Repeated alignments / tire sets

Alignments and tire work appear repeatedly through 89,649 miles in 2021. That shows the car received chassis/tire attention, but it does not tell us the present tires or bushings are good. Read current DOT dates, wear and play directly.

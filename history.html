SYSTEM-BY-SYSTEM

Bumper-to-bumper findings

Engine / ignition

Documented: repeated oil/filter service, several drivability/computer checks, engine-air filters, fuel-system cleaning, and 2026 valve-cover gasket(s).

Not documented: spark plugs, ignition coils, injectors, fuel pump, MAFs, throttle bodies, timing-chain work or internal-engine repair.

Cooling / accessory drive

Not documented: coolant change, radiator, water pump, thermostat, drive belt, tensioner or idlers. At 144k these are inspection/baseline items, not automatic failure assumptions.

Clutch / transmission

Documented: CSC replaced at 56,535 miles.

Not documented: clutch disc, pressure plate, flywheel, master cylinder, transmission repair or 6MT-fluid service.

Differential / steering

Documented: differential fluid/service and power-steering fluid at 73,527 miles.

Not documented: differential bushing, axle/CV, driveshaft, steering rack, pump or tie-rod replacement.

Brakes

Documented: front/rear pads and rear brake service at 55,665 miles; brake-fluid flush/change at 73,527 miles.

Not documented: later pads/rotors/calipers/hoses. Current hard-braking feedback means measure and inspect before buying parts.

Suspension / hubs

No strut/shock, spring, control-arm, bushing, ball-joint, sway-link, hub or wheel-bearing replacement is documented in this CARFAX. That makes the current chassis-health inspection especially useful.

Tires / alignment

Frequent historic alignments and tire installations are documented. The last explicit set is four tires plus alignment at 89,649 miles in 2021. Current tire age/condition must be read from the tires themselves.

Electrical / HVAC

Batteries are documented at 35,568 miles (2016) and 73,527 miles (2019); a light bulb was replaced in 2021. No alternator, starter or A/C repair is documented in the report we have.

Exhaust / emissions

No catalytic-converter, exhaust, O2/A/F-sensor replacement is documented. That matters now because the current catalyst-related CEL should be diagnosed from live evidence rather than assuming an inherited converter repair.

Body / undercarriage

The 2018 event specifically names front and undercarriage minor damage. The 2023 event is also classified minor but the copy we have does not provide a useful location. Inspect the front subframe/supports, undertray, oil pan, steering/rack area, exhaust and low NISMO aero for old repair evidence.

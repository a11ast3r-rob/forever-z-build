Factory wiring strategy

CONFIRMED

Keep the Bose system intact until we choose the complete replacement package. Final system bypasses the Bose amplifier for speaker power and ties the aftermarket amp into the intact factory speaker-side runs to the doors/dash where practical.

reuse OEM runsavoid needless door-jamb pullsverify first

Sound deadening

PART OF THE BUILD

Doors get butyl vibration damping during the component-speaker install, plus foam/decoupling at trim contact points where needed. Hatch/trunk floor and useful resonant panels get treated before the false floor. Rattle-test before final reassembly.

doorshatch / trunkbutyl + foamkeep drains/service access

New wiring + hidden hatch

DO IT RIGHT

New dedicated fused OFC amp power, proper grounds, required signal wiring and new subwoofer speaker wire. Amps, DSP, subs, two hidden air reservoirs, compressors and management are designed together as a removable/serviceable hatch system.

fuse near batterynew sub wireventilationaccessible drains

Dual 8s • DIY enclosure

CURRENT TARGET

Plan around a pair of shallow 8s in a removable sealed enclosure behind the seats / at the forward cargo edge. Leading sub idea: dual KICKER CompRT 8s. Their 300 W RMS each / dual-2Ω configuration can be wired to a 2Ω final load, matching the T1000X5ad's 600 W sub channel almost perfectly.

Project-car rule

DIY FIRST

Take the time to learn and do as much of the build ourselves as makes sense. Measure, template, mock up, wire, deaden and fabricate properly; use specialists only where the job genuinely benefits from specialized equipment or expertise.

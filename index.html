ROADMAP

Build plan

Wheels + tires

PLANNED
  • Gram Lights 57DR Gunblue
  • 18×9.5 +22 / 18×10.5 +12 target
  • Meaty street tire; wet/highway manners matter
  • Final fitment verified on the actual car before ordering

Air suspension

RESEARCH
  • Z34-specific performance air hardware
  • Pressure + height-based management preferred
  • Dual electric compressors
  • Two smaller hidden tanks as one shared reserve
  • Drive / lift / aired-out presets

Geometry

RESEARCH
  • Front camber correction as needed
  • Rear camber correction
  • Rear toe solution compatible with rear air-spring location
  • Street-oriented bushings/joints where sensible
  • Alignment at normal drive height

Audio + treatment

PLANNED
  • Keep/assess upgraded head unit
  • Speaker upgrade
  • Subs are non-negotiable
  • Sound deadening where it adds real value
  • False floor shared intelligently with air management

Cooling

WATCH
  • Baseline temps and maintenance first
  • Thermostatic oil cooler if warranted
  • Clean routing, airflow, and service access

Forever-build details

RULE
  • Hidden/protected wiring and plumbing
  • Threaded service points where appropriate
  • Photograph and label disassembly
  • Custom pieces remain removable/serviceable

BUILD RULES

Non-negotiables

1.It has to drive well, not just photograph well.
2.Impact removes. Torque wrench finishes.
3.Airline and wiring stay protected from heat, abrasion, and movement.
4.Custom work remains removable and serviceable.
5.Drive-height alignment matters more than aired-out alignment.
6.Subs are happening. This is settled law.

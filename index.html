Renew the right stuff, not everything

High-priority rubber

INSPECT
  • Rear differential bushing
  • Front compression/tension bushings
  • Ball joints / tie rods
  • Sway bushings/end links
  • Dust boots and axle boots

Stay soft unless needed

STREET
  • Engine mounts
  • Transmission mount
  • Rear subframe bushings
  • Random chassis bushings

OEM-quality rubber first. Street urethane where control matters. Solid/spherical only with a real reason.

Air suspension

PLANNED

Current architecture: Air Lift front 76010 / rear 76510, ALP4 3/8-inch management, optional height upgrade, dual compressors, two hidden reservoirs as one shared reserve.

Plan for possible front shock-tower clearance work and rear spring-seat drilling. This is a custom install, not a slap-it-on weekend shortcut.

Air Lift 2009 fitment ↗ALP4 ↗

Geometry / bushings

MEASURE FIRST

Street-bushed front/rear adjustability only where alignment needs it. Rear toe correction must preserve the factory spring bucket for the planned rear Air Lift layout.

SPC 72265 ↗Z1 FUCAs ↗Z1 street suspension kit ↗

Differential

VERIFY

Inspect the fluid-filled rear diff bushing early. If failed, current preference is quality street urethane rather than solid aluminum. If the car proves open-diff, a quiet street-friendly LSD becomes a high-value later fun upgrade.

Z1 urethane diff bushing ↗

Cooling

LIKELY

Baseline engine health first, then a thermostatic external oil cooler is one of the strongest likely longevity upgrades for spirited summer use. Healthy OEM radiator/fans stay.

2009 long-term heat history ↗Metal heater-hose bleeder example ↗

Audio + hidden air hatch

DESIGN TOGETHER

Identify the current head unit and existing wiring first. Then speakers + amp(s) + subs + sound treatment + manifold + tanks + compressors become one removable/serviceable hatch architecture.

  • No mystery splices
  • Main fuse near power source
  • Proper grounds
  • Compressor ventilation
  • Accessible drains
  • Keep useful cargo space

Wrap / exterior

FUTURE

Pearl White is only the starting point. PDR, cracks, chips, paint failure and panel alignment get handled before wrap. Paint can remain a far-future decision.

Brakes

DECIDE BEFORE WHEELS

If physically verified as base brakes, good pads/rotors/fluid may be all we need initially. An Akebono/Sport-style conversion remains a nice OEM+ path if we want thermal capacity and looks.

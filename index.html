ROADMAP

Build plan

Nothing here means “purchased” unless marked owned. This is the living plan.

Stage 0 — NISMO baseline

FIRST
  • Full lift inspection on arrival, especially the documented 2018 front/undercarriage area
  • Establish fluid, tire, brake, battery/charging and leak baseline
  • Check wheel bearings, bushings, engine/trans mounts and suspension wear
  • Verify clutch/CSC behavior, gearbox synchros and differential condition
  • Confirm cooling health before power/fun mods
  • Keep a dated service log from day one

Factory NISMO hardware

PRESERVE
  • NISMO-tuned shocks, springs and stabilizer bars
  • NISMO front strut brace + performance body dampers
  • Nissan sport brakes with red calipers
  • Viscous limited-slip differential
  • NISMO free-flow dual exhaust
  • Do not replace good NISMO parts unless the new setup is a real upgrade

Wheels + tires

PLANNED
  • Gram Lights 57DR in Gunblue
  • 18×9.5 +22 front
  • 18×10.5 +12 rear
  • 255/40R18 front / 285/35R18 rear target
  • Highway-friendly tire; no silly stretch
  • Keep the original NISMO wheels with the car

Air suspension

RESEARCH
  • Air Lift Performance Z34 front + rear hardware
  • Modern pressure/height management
  • Dual electric compressors
  • Two smaller hidden tanks instead of one display tank
  • Drive / lift / aired-out presets

Suspension geometry

RESEARCH
  • Adjustable front upper control arms
  • Rear camber correction
  • Rear toe correction compatible with air spring location
  • Street-oriented joints/bushings where possible
  • Final alignment at normal drive height

Audio + sound treatment

PLANNED
  • Keep upgraded head unit
  • Replace speakers
  • Subs are non-negotiable 😂
  • Sound deadening in hatch/doors as useful
  • False-floor design shared with hidden air system

Cooling + longevity

BASELINE
  • Inspect baseline temps, hoses, fans, coolant condition and service history first
  • Thermostatic oil cooler if warranted by use/temps
  • Refresh age/wear items before chasing power
  • Install every cooling upgrade cleanly with serviceability in mind

Forever-build details

  • Rivnuts/threaded service points where appropriate
  • Photograph and label disassembly
  • Make every custom component removable/serviceable

BUILD RULES

Things we're not compromising

1.It has to drive well, not just photograph well.
2.Critical fasteners get torqued properly. Impact ≠ torque wrench.
3.Airline and wiring stay protected from heat, abrasion and moving parts.
4.Custom work remains serviceable and removable.
5.The final drive-height alignment matters more than the aired-out alignment.
6.Subs are happening. This is settled law.
7.Factory NISMO parts stay unless the replacement is a measurable, intentional upgrade.
8.Baseline reliability and wear items come before power mods.

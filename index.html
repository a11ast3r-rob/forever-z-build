KEEP IT TIGHT

Longevity + fun plan

The chassis strategy is renew first, selectively upgrade second. We want zero slop and good geometry without turning the car into an NVH machine.

Bushings / joints

  • Inspect rear differential bushing early
  • Inspect front compression/tension bushings
  • Ball joints, tie rods, sway links, wheel bearings
  • Rear subframe stays stock if healthy

Driveline

  • Clutch/CSC health first
  • Manual + differential fluid baseline
  • Engine/trans mounts only if worn
  • LSD later if the car is open-diff

Cooling / monitoring

  • Thermostatic oil cooler planned
  • Healthy stock radiator stays
  • Oil pressure/consumption baseline
  • Protect undertray and airflow

Street-first materials

  • OEM rubber where comfort matters
  • Street urethane where control matters
  • Solid/spherical only with a reason

Fun after healthy

  • 57DRs + good tires
  • Bags + proper alignment
  • Exhaust, brakes, audio
  • Wrap after body prep

No parts-cannon

  • Do not replace sensors, hubs, coils, mounts or radiator without evidence
  • Test, document, then spend

BUILD RULES

Non-negotiables

1.It has to drive well, not just photograph well.
2.Impact removes. Torque wrench finishes.
3.Airline and wiring stay protected from heat, abrasion, and movement.
4.Custom work remains removable and serviceable.
5.Drive-height alignment matters more than aired-out alignment.
6.Subs are happening. This is settled law.

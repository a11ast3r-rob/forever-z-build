# Forever Z — End-to-End Checklist

This is the master checklist from **candidate car** to **fully documented forever build**.

The rule is simple: **learn the car before changing the car**.

---

## Status gates

Use these three states whenever we learn something new:

- **STOP / FIX NOW** — safety, reliability, fluid loss, bad tire/brake, serious electrical, or anything that can strand/damage the car.
- **DRIVE / MONITOR** — acceptable for now, but log symptoms, measurements, dates, and mileage.
- **READY TO MODIFY** — baseline is understood well enough that the next modification will not hide an existing problem.

# The build order

1. **Buy / verify the right car**
2. **Document the car exactly as received**
3. **Inventory everything already modified**
4. **Make it safe and reliable**
5. **Drive it enough to learn it**
6. **Measure it**
7. **Lock wheel / tire / suspension geometry**
8. **Design air + audio packaging together**
9. **Add cooling only if actual use warrants it**
10. **Wrap after the body is ready**
11. **Log every change forever**

That order is what keeps this from turning into a pile of cool parts attached to an unknown car.

# 144k Mechanical Baseline — 2013 370Z NISMO

Updated **2026-09-28**.

## Principle

Inspect first. Establish unknown maintenance clocks. Replace confirmed wear. The odometer is a reason to look closely, not permission to buy everything.

## Immediate evidence

### CEL / catalyst
- Save exact catalytic-converter-related DTC(s), status and freeze frame before clearing.
- Comers found the driver-side exhaust flow lower than the other side but found no obvious major fault.
- Shop suggested a Sea Foam trial.
- Do not call the converter failed until repeat data/diagnosis supports it.
- Compare banks for fuel trims, O2 behavior and available misfire information.
- Fix upstream fueling/misfire/air-leak causes before replacing any converter.

### Engine
- Recheck the fresh 2026-09-11 valve-cover-gasket repair for seepage.
- Inspect all six plug wells when the plugs come out; they should be dry after the fresh gasket work.
- Oil level/condition and consumption log.
- Belt, tensioner/idlers and PCV system.
- Intake tubes/airboxes/MAF condition.
- Cooling-system pressure/condition check.
- Battery/charging and engine/chassis grounds.
- Compression/leak-down only if symptoms or evidence justify escalation.

## Easy / cheap baseline items

### Do / strongly consider
- Engine air filters ×2.
- Cabin air filter.
- Accessory belt if age is unknown or condition is not excellent.
- PCV valves/hoses by condition.
- **Spark plugs ×6 with the correct quality/OE-spec part.**

### Spark plugs / coils

Nissan specifies **60,000 miles / 48 months** for 2013 NISMO spark plugs.

Because no replacement is documented for our VIN in the records we have, establish our own baseline now rather than assuming the ~60k or ~120k service happened.

Coils are different: do **not** replace all six simply because of mileage. Inspect boots/connectors, look for oil contamination, and use cylinder-specific misfire evidence to justify replacement.

## Fluids / clocks

Verify records, then establish our own baseline as needed:
- 6MT fluid,
- differential fluid,
- coolant,
- power steering,
- brake fluid,
- clutch hydraulic fluid.

For the 2013 NISMO brake circuit, Nissan specifies **R35 Special II** factory-fill fluid. The clutch reservoir spec is separate.

## Chassis / brakes

Current road evidence:
- good highway cruising,
- no steering-wheel vibration at speed,
- rough/semi-jarring response on normal roads,
- some harder-braking feedback.

Wheels-off inspection:
- tire pressure and wear,
- shocks/dampers, bump stops, mounts,
- control-arm bushings,
- ball joints,
- tie rods/rack play,
- sway links/bushings,
- rear links/bushings,
- rear differential bushing,
- hubs/wheel bearings,
- pads/rotors/calipers/hoses,
- rotor runout/thickness variation only if symptoms call for it.

Cheap sway bushings/end links are good repairs **if worn**. Rear diff bushing is a high-priority check but not an easy “just replace it” job.

## Cooling / oil

Observed oil temperature: about **220°F** during ownership driving.

Current rule:
- log ambient + traffic/highway conditions,
- verify radiator/fans/hoses/coolant condition,
- add an OEM-style oil-pressure gauge,
- later install a thermostatic 25-row oil cooler for margin once the baseline is healthy.

## Air build gate

Do not buy the full air system until:
- unsafe/sloppy chassis faults are fixed,
- alignment hardware/geometry needs are understood,
- hatch packaging is measured against audio plans,
- normal drive height and clearance goals are defined.

Default remains a vehicle-specific divorced rear layout with stealth management under the hatch floor.

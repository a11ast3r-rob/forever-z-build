## Tires

**Current size direction**
- Front: ~255/40R18
- Rear: ~285/35R18

**Diameter note**
- Compared with the factory Base 225/50R18 + 245/45R18 tires, this pair is about 3.1% shorter overall.
- The front/rear diameter relationship remains almost identical to stock, which is good.
- Keep this as a target, then compare taller alternatives after actual air-suspension, fender, brake and DRIVE-height measurements.

Priorities:
- Highway stability
- Wet grip
- Useful sidewall
- No extreme stretch
- Reasonable tire life for a performance tire

## Cooling

### Current oil-cooler target — verified 2026-09-21

**Z1 Motorsports 370Z / G37 Oil Cooler Kit**
- Product: https://www.z1motorsports.com/z1-products/z1-motorsports/z1-motorsports-370z-g37-oil-cooler-kit-p-4135.html
- Fits 2009+ 370Z and NISMO models; Z1's 2015+ NISMO brace warning does not apply to our 2013.
- Configure as:
  - **25-row core**
  - Prefer **Setrab Series 6** if the configured price is sensible; Z1 25-row core is the value fallback
  - **197°F / 92°C thermostatic sandwich plate**
  - **-10AN braided stainless lines**
  - **protective hose wrapping**
- Z1 lists the base kit at **$449.99 before selected upgrades/options** as of 2026-09-21.
- Keep the factory 2012+ coolant-to-oil heat exchanger if it is healthy and packaging allows. Z1 says most owners retain it and cooling is best with it, though clearance is tighter.
- Expect roughly one extra quart on the first fill depending on configuration; verify final oil level after startup/circulation instead of filling by guess.

**Why 25-row**
- Z1 describes 19-row for light street/cold-climate use.
- Z1 describes 25-row for more intense use / occasional track use / moderate climates.
- Z1 points 34-row toward forced induction, serious track work or high-temperature use.
- Our naturally aspirated, street/highway, high-mileage NISMO needs controlled margin, not maximum core size.
- A thermostat is not optional for our plan; Z1 recommends thermostatic plates on daily-driven cars.

- https://www.z1motorsports.com/z1-products/z1-motorsports/z1-370z-g37-silicone-radiator-hose-set-p-3716.html
- Current reference price: **$129.99**.
- Condition/age-based, not an automatic purchase if the existing hoses are genuinely healthy.

**Coolant**
- Nissan's 2013 maintenance guide: factory-fill Genuine Nissan Long Life Blue coolant is due at 105,000 miles / 7 years; subsequent replacement is every 75,000 miles / 5 years.
- If current coolant age cannot be documented, reset that clock during baseline service.
- Use Genuine Nissan Blue or an equivalent meeting the required specification. Do not add stop-leak/sealer or random cooling additives.

### Radiator direction

**Keep the OEM radiator if healthy.** Pressure-test it, inspect fins/end tanks and verify both fans before replacing anything.

If an upgraded replacement is actually justified:
- **Mishimoto MMRAD-370Z-09**
- https://www.mishimoto.com/nissan-370z-performance-aluminum-radiator-09.html
- Current reference price: **$732.95**.
- Direct-fit 2009-2020 370Z, two-row 1.57-inch core, and includes an incorporated A/C condenser.
- Correct direction for our 6MT daily if the radiator needs replacement.

**Do not buy:** Mishimoto **MMRAD-370Z-09TR** for this build. It is the track-focused version and deletes the A/C condenser.

Also do not buy a low-temperature engine thermostat simply to force a lower gauge number. We want stable designed operating temperature, not chronic overcooling.

### Temperature rule

Nissan states normal oil temperature remains below **280°F (140°C)** and instructs the driver to reduce RPM if the gauge reaches 280°F or above. We will log real oil temperature before and after the cooler; the cooler is thermal margin, not a license to ignore an underlying cooling-system fault.

### Wiring / acoustic treatment

- Keep factory Bose integration intact during the radio stage.
- When adding the aftermarket front amp, intentionally preserve the Bose sub signal path until the sub is actually replaced.
- Reuse healthy factory speaker-side runs where practical.
- Use dedicated fused OFC power / proper ground for aftermarket amplification.
- Treat the doors during the component install.
- Use butyl damping and foam / decoupling where it solves resonance.
- Preserve drains, service holes, trim fasteners and wiring access.
- Treat the hatch only where useful; the new stealth-single-10 direction may avoid a large false-floor audio build.

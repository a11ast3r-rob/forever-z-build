# Parts & Fitment Notebook

Nothing in this file is considered purchased unless explicitly marked **OWNED**.

## Wheels

**Current target**
- RAYS Gram Lights 57DR
- Finish: Gunblue
- Front target: 18×9.5 +22
- Rear target: 18×10.5 +12

**Audit note**
- The exact 57DR 18×9.5 +22 / 18×10.5 +12 wheel pair is documented on real 370Z builds.
- A documented 370Z also runs the exact 255/40R18 + 285/35R18 tire sizes on those wheel specs without rubbing on coilovers.
- This is evidence the concept is real, **not proof of Air Lift clearance on our car**.
- Brake path (base vs future Akebono) must be decided/verified before ordering.

## Tires

**Current size direction**
- Front: ~255/40R18
- Rear: ~285/35R18

**Diameter note**
- Compared with the factory Base 225/50R18 + 245/45R18 tires, this pair is about 3.1% shorter overall.
- The front/rear diameter relationship remains almost identical to stock, which is good.
- Keep this as a target, then compare taller alternatives after actual air-suspension, fender, brake and DRIVE-height measurements.

Priorities:
- Highway stability
- Wet grip
- Useful sidewall
- No extreme stretch
- Reasonable tire life for a performance tire

## Air suspension

Current architecture:
- Air Lift Performance front 76010 / rear 76510 current fitment direction
- Air Lift notes possible front shock-tower indenting and rear upper/lower spring-seat drilling on the 2009 370Z
- ALP4 3/8-inch management 27485 current management direction
- Optional pressure-to-height upgrade 27705
- Modern pressure/height management
- Dual electric compressors
- Two smaller hidden reservoirs connected as a common reserve
- Drive / lift / aired-out presets

## Alignment / geometry

Expected categories:
- Adjustable front camber correction
- Rear camber correction
- Rear toe correction compatible with the rear air-spring layout (SPC 72265 remains a candidate)
- Do not use true-style toe arms that delete the factory spring bucket with the planned rear Air Lift layout
- Optional geometry links only if measurements justify them

## Cooling

Oil cooler remains a conditional but high-value likely item:
- Baseline engine health and oil-temperature behavior first
- Thermostatic external setup preferred for a street-driven car if added
- Do not replace a healthy factory radiator/fans without measured need

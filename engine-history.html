WHAT THIS CHANGES

Our engine plan now

1 · PlugsNISMO interval is 60k / 48 months. No replacement is documented for this VIN, so install six correct-quality plugs and start our own clock.
2 · CoilsDo not buy six automatically. Inspect boots/connectors and use scanner/misfire evidence to justify replacement.
3 · Plug wellsThe valve-cover gaskets are fresh. When plugs come out, verify the wells are dry and the repair is staying sealed.
4 · CEL evidenceSave DTCs/freeze frame and compare banks. Any misfire/fueling/air problem gets fixed before condemning a catalyst.
5 · Keep loggingRecord every service from here forward so the Forever Z never has this history ambiguity again.
